Limits

What this study can and cannot claim

  • Effects on reviews and lateness come from observational data with controls for state, category, order value and month. They are associations, which is why the recommendation is a randomized test.
  • The dataset covers one Brazilian marketplace in 2017–18. Payment methods such as boleto are local; the Canadian parallel is an argument by analogy.
  • Review themes come from keyword rules in Portuguese. They were checked on a held-out sample, but the sample is small.
  • The experiment is designed, not run. Its power check uses resampled historical orders.
